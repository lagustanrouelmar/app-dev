# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Firebird*

## About the Movie
**Firebird** is a 2021 LGBTQ+ romantic drama directed by **Peeter Rebane**. It is based on the memoir *The Story of Roman* by Sergey Fetisov. The story takes place in 1977 at a Soviet Air Force base in Estonia.

![Firebird movie poster](https://via.placeholder.com/300x450.png?text=Firebird+Poster)

## Movie Details

| **Setting** | Soviet-occupied Estonia, 1977 |
| **Language** | English |

## Main Characters
1. **Sergey** – a young conscript who dreams of becoming an actor
2. **Roman** – a skilled and ambitious fighter pilot
3. **Luisa** – a secretary at the base and Sergey's friend

## Why I Love This Movie
- It tells a story of **love** under dangerous circumstances
- It shows the struggle of *hiding who you are*
- The cinematography is beautiful
  - The snowy air base scenes
  - The emotional close-ups
- It is based on a **true story**

## Themes in the Movie
